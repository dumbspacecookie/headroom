  - **The research found our baseline was weaker than public practice.** `reasonable` never nets out energy it already admitted; Sunverge's patent and ERCOT's NPRR 1186 both do. The 6.4% vs -15.8% headline mostly measured the standard part.

Three things, found one under another:
  - **The oracle had no per-device check at all.** It now runs stage 2 as "n0": every booking deliverable per device, no region removed.
